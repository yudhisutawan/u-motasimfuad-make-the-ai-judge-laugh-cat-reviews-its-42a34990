One funny single-line one-star review from a house cat about its human,
under 200 characters, for the
[make.fun competition](https://make.fun/competitions/u-motasimfuad-make-the-ai-judge-laugh-cat-reviews-its-42a34990)
"Make the AI Judge Laugh: Cat Reviews Its Human".

## The entry

> ★☆☆☆☆ Bottom of porcelain disk visible through dry kibble. Servant acted as though I had eaten an hour ago. Starved to death twice before breakfast.

148 characters, one line.

## How it was chosen

18 candidates were written (10 local, 8 from Hermes — see
`CANDIDATES-HERMES.md`), scored against the judge's five published criteria,
then the top 6 were judged head-to-head in a 15-match pairwise round robin.
The winner swept 5–0. Full results in `TOURNAMENT.md`.

## Running

```sh
MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=/tmp/out python3 -S -P main.py
cat /tmp/out/entry.txt
```

`main.py` is stdlib-only and writes the winning line to
`$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt`.
